ARIEL ON TOUR
[activation] [2025]

With the launch of its new laundry sheets, Ariel decided to break the mold by transforming the everyday chore of doing laundry into a cultural, pop, and unexpected experience.

To create surprise, the brand imagined “Ariel,” a fake international pop icon, a star who doesn't exist... but who will set social media abuzz as if she were real. Ariel launched a fictional tour, “Ariel On Tour,” where each laundry sheet became a ticket to a musical, fun, and exaggeratedly pop universe.

The activation comes to life on Instagram through a series of vox pops where people are introduced to the “new star.” Passersby are invited to listen to AI-generated song excerpts. Each of Ariel's fictional “albums” corresponds to a new artistic direction and a new fragrance.

Work in collaboration with Eglantine Duflot, Mya Hédin, and Cécile Cabel

This campaign is conducted in a fictional setting.
